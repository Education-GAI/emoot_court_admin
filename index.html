What these metrics tell you

Active users: unique students, judges and admins using NEXUS in the selected period; monitor adoption and find institutions that may need onboarding. Executed sessions: completed moot courts; use it to plan platform capacity. Credits consumed: usage charged against included allowances and prepaid wallets; use it to forecast usage and top-up demand. Gross accrued revenue: subscription fees plus prepaid credit purchases; use it to monitor commercial performance. These figures are sample data; a production dashboard can calculate them from workspace, session, usage-meter and payment records.
